# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

**EN** — This Code of Conduct applies to every CoreTrees repository, issue, pull request, and community space.<br/>
**ID** — Kode Etik ini berlaku untuk setiap repositori, issue, pull request, dan ruang komunitas CoreTrees.

## Our Pledge · Ikrar Kami

**EN** — We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

**ID** — Kami sebagai anggota, kontributor, dan pemimpin berikrar untuk menjadikan partisipasi dalam komunitas kami sebagai pengalaman yang bebas dari pelecehan bagi semua orang, tanpa memandang usia, ukuran tubuh, disabilitas yang terlihat maupun tidak, etnis, karakteristik seks, identitas dan ekspresi gender, tingkat pengalaman, pendidikan, status sosial-ekonomi, kebangsaan, penampilan, ras, agama, atau identitas dan orientasi seksual.

---

## Our Standards · Standar Kami

**Positive examples · Contoh positif** — behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

**Empathy & kindness** — demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people. · Menunjukkan empati dan kebaikan.<br/>
**Respect** — being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences. · Menghormati perbedaan pendapat dan pengalaman.<br/>
**Constructive feedback** — giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback. · Memberi dan menerima umpan balik konstruktif dengan lapang dada.<br/>
**Accountability** — accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes. · Bertanggung jawab dan meminta maaf atas kesalahan.<br/>
**Community focus** — focusing on what is best for the overall community. · Mengutamakan yang terbaik bagi komunitas.

**Unacceptable examples · Contoh tidak dapat diterima**:

**Sexualized content** — sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind. · Bahasa/citra seksual dan rayuan seksual.<br/>
**Trolling & attacks** — trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks. · Trolling, hinaan, serangan pribadi/politik.<br/>
**Harassment** — public or private harassment. · Pelecehan publik maupun privat.<br/>
**Privacy violations** — publishing others' private information without explicit permission. · Menyebarkan informasi pribadi orang lain tanpa izin.<br/>
**Other misconduct** — other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting. · Perilaku lain yang tidak pantas secara profesional.

## Enforcement Responsibilities · Tanggung Jawab Penegakan

**EN** — Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

**ID** — Pemimpin komunitas bertanggung jawab menjelaskan dan menegakkan standar ini serta akan mengambil tindakan korektif yang adil terhadap perilaku yang dinilai tidak pantas, mengancam, ofensif, atau merugikan.

## Enforcement · Penegakan

**EN** — Instances of abuse or unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the maintainer listed in [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md), via their GitHub profile. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

**ID** — Laporan pelecehan atau perilaku yang tidak dapat diterima dapat dikirim secara privat kepada maintainer yang tercantum di [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md), melalui profil GitHub mereka. Semua laporan akan ditinjau dan ditindaklanjuti secara cepat dan adil, dengan menjaga privasi dan keamanan pelapor.

## Enforcement Guidelines · Panduan Penegakan

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines · Pemimpin komunitas mengikuti panduan dampak berikut:

**1. Correction · Koreksi** — private, written warning with clarity around the violation.<br/>
**2. Warning · Peringatan** — a warning with consequences for continued behavior.<br/>
**3. Temporary Ban · Larangan Sementara** — a temporary ban from interaction.<br/>
**4. Permanent Ban · Larangan Permanen** — a permanent ban from the community.
