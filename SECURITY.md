# Security Policy · Kebijakan Keamanan

**EN** — We take the security of CoreTrees projects seriously. Thank you for helping keep our users and systems safe.<br/>
**ID** — Kami menanggapi keamanan proyek CoreTrees dengan serius. Terima kasih telah membantu menjaga keamanan pengguna dan sistem kami.

---

## Reporting a Vulnerability · Melaporkan Kerentanan

**Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues.**<br/>
**Mohon jangan melaporkan kerentanan melalui GitHub issue publik.**

Use **GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting**: open the repository's **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**.<br/>
Gunakan **GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting**: buka tab **Security** repositori → **Report a vulnerability**.

Please include · Mohon sertakan:

**Description & impact** — what the issue is and its potential impact. · Deskripsi masalah dan dampaknya.<br/>
**Reproduction** — steps to reproduce, a proof-of-concept if possible. · Langkah reproduksi (PoC jika ada).<br/>
**Scope** — affected version(s), component, or endpoint. · Versi/komponen/endpoint yang terdampak.<br/>
**Suggested fix** — any remediation ideas you may have. · Saran perbaikan (jika ada).

---

## Our Commitment · Komitmen Kami

| Stage · Tahap | Target |
|---|---|
| Acknowledge report · Konfirmasi laporan | within **48 hours** · dalam **48 jam** |
| Initial assessment · Penilaian awal | within **5 business days** · dalam **5 hari kerja** |
| Fix & disclosure plan · Rencana perbaikan & pengungkapan | coordinated with reporter · dikoordinasikan dengan pelapor |

**EN** — We follow **coordinated disclosure**: we will work with you on a fix and a public disclosure timeline, and credit you (if you wish) once the issue is resolved.<br/>
**ID** — Kami menerapkan **coordinated disclosure**: kami akan bekerja sama dengan Anda untuk perbaikan dan jadwal pengungkapan publik, serta memberi kredit (jika Anda mau) setelah selesai.

---

## Supported Versions · Versi yang Didukung

Unless stated otherwise in a repository, security fixes target the **latest released version** on the default branch.<br/>
Kecuali dinyatakan lain, perbaikan keamanan menargetkan **versi rilis terbaru** pada default branch.

## Scope · Ruang Lingkup

**In scope · Termasuk** — source code in CoreTrees repositories, build and CI configuration, and documented deployment manifests.<br/>
**Out of scope · Tidak termasuk** — third-party services and dependencies (report upstream), and findings that require physical access or already-compromised accounts.

## Please avoid · Mohon hindari

**Data access** — accessing or modifying data that isn't yours. · Mengakses/mengubah data milik orang lain.<br/>
**Service disruption** — degrading service for other users; no DoS testing. · Mengganggu layanan pengguna lain (tanpa DoS).<br/>
**Social engineering** — phishing, pretexting, or physical attacks. · Rekayasa sosial, phishing, atau serangan fisik.
